Vikash Sharma, born on October 11, 2008, in a village about 15 kilometers from Jaipur on the Ajmer highway, is a remarkable individual. Although blind since birth, Vikash's determination and intelligence have allowed him to excel in many areas. He lived in a blind school hostel from 2019 to 2020 and again in 2022 to 2023, accumulating two years of experience there. However, most of his life has been spent at home. Despite these challenges, he passed his 10th board exams through open schooling with impressive marks and is now pursuing his 11th grade studies in a regular school, with the support of home tutors.

Vikash's technical expertise is truly inspiring. Before even starting his formal courses, he had already gained a deep understanding of computers, including operations such as web surfing, installations, system variables, and setting up computers for accessibility. His journey in programming began when he joined the Kotlin language course at V I D Academy, where he excelled and completed the course swiftly. His passion for technology led him to enroll in Android development, followed by a web development course, both of which he completed successfully despite the time constraints imposed by his 10th board exams.

Moreover, Vikash has played a crucial role in V I D Tech Help Group, managing both the group administration and verification departments, showcasing his leadership abilities.
